The Opportunity

Three pressures, one solution

Regulation

EU Green Deal & Fit-for-55 force cities to cut emissions and manage heat & stormwater.

Livability

Heat islands, air quality, and flooding are now top political priorities.

ESG capital

Developers and corporates need credible, visible sustainability assets.

Green infrastructure sits at the intersection of all three — but BLUMA had the product, not the go-to-market.

The Problem

Good product. No repeatable motion.

  • Strong in Lithuania, unknown across the EU.
  • Bought by three very different buyers — government, developers, hospitality — each needing a different message.
  • No segmented GTM motion and no quantified value story to justify the spend.
The Strategy

Quantify → Activate → Scale

1 · Quantify

Build an impact model turning green m² into € savings + CO₂ — a budget-justifiable line item.

2 · Activate

Café/hospitality rollout: rooftop & wall plantations + branded cups = visible, photogenic proof.

3 · Scale

Phase the expansion market-by-market with a B2G + B2B motion that funds the next ring.

The Numbers

€56M toll relief — modal shift

Green corridors make streets walkable, shifting some car trips to walking, cycling and transit. That reduces what commuters pay in tolls and congestion charges.

1,400,000 commuters × €400/yr × 10% modal shift = €56.0M / yr

Conservative: halving the shift to 5% still returns €28M. No single optimistic input carries the result.

Go-To-Market

Prove

  • Calibrate the model
Phase II · Baltics+Nordics

Replicate

  • Recruit delivery partners
Phase III · EU core

Scale

  • Framework / multi-city deals

Each phase has an entry gate and exit KPIs. Spend follows proof, not hope.
