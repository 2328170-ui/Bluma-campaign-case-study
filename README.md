A multi-phase marketing & go-to-market campaign that takes **BLUMA** — a green-infrastructure
brand (green roofs, living walls, urban vegetation) — from its home market in Lithuania across
the European Union. The project pairs a market-entry strategy with a **fully transparent,
interactive impact model** that quantifies the environmental and fiscal case for green infrastructure.

> 📅 Originally a group project (Apr–May 2023), rebuilt here as a structured portfolio artifact.

---

## 📊 Headline Results *(modeled projections at full European scale)*

| Metric | Value | Derivation |
|---|---|---|
| 🏛️ Government savings | **~€148.76M / yr** | Stormwater + energy + health + amenity + carbon |
| 🚗 Toll / congestion relief | **~€56M / yr** | Green-corridor modal shift |
| 🌍 CO₂ sequestered | **~18,000 t / yr** | 6 kg/m² across 3,000,000 m² |
| 🗺️ Rollout | **Phase I–III** | Lithuania → Baltics → EU core |

All figures are **transparent, adjustable projections** — every assumption is exposed in the
interactive model. The math reconciles exactly to the headline numbers (verified).

## ✨ What's Inside

- **📈 Interactive impact model** — a single-file browser calculator. Drag the sliders and watch
  €-savings, toll relief, and CO₂ recompute live. Built to be *defended*, not just presented.
- **🧭 Market-expansion strategy** — a "rings of similarity" expansion logic with market sizing.
- **🗺️ Phase I–III roadmap** — each phase has an entry gate and exit KPIs; spend follows proof.
- **🎯 ICP & personas** — three buyer segments (B2G, B2B, hospitality), each with its own value story.
- **📨 SDR playbook** — outreach sequences, cold-email scripts, qualification, objection handling.
- **☕ Hospitality activation** — café selection scorecard, branded cups, rooftop/wall plantations.
- **🎤 Pitch deck** — a 10-slide, keyboard-navigable browser presentation.

## 🧮 Methodology — How the Numbers Work

The impact model sizes by **green surface area** (the product is priced per m²) rather than population.

**Footprint:** 15 cities × 200,000 m² convertible roof/wall area = **3,000,000 m²**

**Government savings (€/m²/yr × footprint):**

| Category | €/m²/yr | Annual value |
|---|---|---|
| Stormwater management | €15 | €45.00M |
| Energy / urban cooling | €10 | €30.00M |
| Public health (air quality) | €20 | €60.00M |
| Biodiversity / amenity | €4 | €12.00M |
| Carbon (18,000 t × €98/t) | — | €1.76M |
| **Total** | | **€148.76M** |

**Toll relief:** `1,400,000 commuters × €400/yr × 10% modal shift = €56M`

The headline conclusion is **robust to its assumptions** — carbon is <2% of the total, so even
halving CO₂ figures barely moves the result. Full derivation in [`docs/04-impact-model.md`](docs/04-impact-model.md).
