# Hospitality Rollout — The Awareness Engine

The hospitality channel is **not** the revenue driver. It is the cheapest, fastest way to put
green infrastructure in front of thousands of people in high-footfall locations — creating the
physical, photogenic proof that warms up every B2G and B2B conversation.

> **Strategic role:** Hospitality is top-of-funnel for the *whole brand*. A living wall on a
> popular café does more for awareness than any ad — and it's a working product demo.

---

## Why cafés

| Reason | Effect |
|---|---|
| **High footfall** | Thousands of daily impressions per site |
| **Photo-friendly** | Customers post → free organic reach |
| **Fast decision** | Owner = decision-maker; short sales cycle |
| **Visible product demo** | A real, maintained install prospects can visit |
| **Brand halo** | "As seen at {popular café}" credibility for bigger buyers |

---

## Café selection criteria (scorecard)

Score each candidate 1–5; prioritize the highest totals.

| Criterion | What we want | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Footfall / location | Central, high pedestrian traffic | ×3 |
| Usable surface | Rooftop, courtyard, or street-facing wall | ×3 |
| Brand fit | Owner competes on vibe/sustainability | ×2 |
| Visibility | Surface seen from street / social-friendly | ×2 |
| Owner enthusiasm | Wants to co-promote | ×1 |

**Target:** 5 flagship sites in Phase I (Vilnius), then 3–5 per new capital in Phase II.

---

## The three deliverables per site

1. **Rooftop or wall plantation** — a modular green roof or living wall sized to the surface.
   The hero asset. Designed to look good in every season and be low-hassle (we maintain it).
2. **Branded cups** — BLUMA-branded compostable cups carrying a QR code to the impact-model
   microsite. Every coffee becomes a mini-billboard that travels out of the café.
3. **In-store story card** — a small placard: "This wall removes X kg CO₂/yr and cools this
   block." Turns the install into an explainer and a talking point.

---

## Activation playbook per site

```
Week 0   Sign café (scorecard-qualified) + agree co-promotion
Week 1   Design the wall/roof + mock up branded cups
Week 2   Install (modular = fast) + place story card
Week 3   Launch moment: local press + owner's socials + BLUMA channels
Ongoing  Maintenance visits + collect content (before/after, footfall anecdotes)
```

```
       Café install (visible, photographed)

     ┌───────────┼─────────────┐
     ▼           ▼             ▼
  Organic     Branded-cup    "As seen at"
  social      QR → model     proof for B2G
  reach        microsite      & B2B pitches
     │           │             │
     └────► warmer outbound + inbound leads ◄────┘
```

- **For B2G (Daiva):** "Come see a live BLUMA wall at {café} downtown" beats a slide deck.
- **For B2B (Marek):** "Customers are posting about it" demonstrates the tenant-appeal claim.
- **For the SDR team:** café installs become the proof point and conversation-opener in
  Sequences A and B (see `06-sdr-playbook.md`).

## Cost/benefit framing

The hospitality program is run as a **marketing budget line**, not a profit center. Compared
to paid media, a flagship café install delivers:

- A permanent, maintained physical demo (vs. a temporary ad).
- Organic, compounding social reach (vs. paid impressions that stop when spend stops).
- A credibility asset reused across every other segment.

> KPI for this channel: **impressions + social reach + leads-influenced**, not direct café revenue.
