# SDR Outreach Playbook

This turns the strategy and impact model into a **repeatable outbound motion**: how to find
accounts, sequence touches, what to say, and how to handle objections. Built for the SDR role.

---

## 1. The motion in 6 steps

```
1. Source     → build account list from ICP triggers (05-icp-personas.md)
2. Research   → find the trigger + the persona's KPI
3. Sequence   → multi-touch outreach (email + LinkedIn + call)
4. Qualify    → BANT-lite: budget line, authority, mandate, timeline
5. Book       → hand a qualified meeting to the AE / founder
6. Log        → CRM hygiene + update conversion benchmarks
```

**SDR success metric:** qualified meetings booked per segment, not emails sent.

---

## 2. Targeting & list-building

| Segment | Where to source | Qualifying signals |
|---|---|---|
| B2G | City climate-plan PDFs, EU funding announcements, gov org charts, LinkedIn (sustainability/public-works titles) | Published plan, allocated budget, recent flood/heat event |
| B2B | Property/dev news, REIT portfolios, ESG report mentions, LinkedIn (ESG/development titles) | New development, ESG commitment, certification target |
| Hospitality | Maps/footfall, local press, Instagram-popular venues with usable surfaces | Has rooftop/wall, brand-led positioning, recent opening |

## 3. Outreach sequences

### Sequence A — B2G (Daiva, Head of Urban Sustainability)
> Tone: peer-to-peer, public-value framing, lead with the model, ask for a short call.

**Day 1 — Email 1 (the hook)**
```
Subject: green roofs → ~€X public savings for {City}

Hi {Name},

Saw {City}'s {climate plan / adaptation funding} — congrats on {specific detail}.

We model the public value of green infrastructure (stormwater, health, cooling, carbon).
For a footprint the size of {City}, the model points to material annual public savings —
not just emissions, but avoided drainage and health costs.

Worth 15 minutes to walk you through the model for {City}? I can send the calculator first.

{Sig}
```

**Day 1 — LinkedIn:** connect, no pitch ("Following {City}'s climate work — would love to connect.")

**Day 3 — Email 2 (proof):**
```
Subject: re: green roofs → public savings for {City}

Quick add — here's a 1-page version of the impact model and a before/after from {reference}.
The interesting part for {City} is {stormwater / heat}, given {trigger}. Open to a look?
```

**Day 6 — Call + voicemail:** reference the email, offer two time slots.

**Day 9 — LinkedIn message:** share the interactive calculator link.

**Day 13 — Email 3 (break-up):**
```
Subject: should I close the loop on {City}?

Happy to step back if the timing's off. If green infrastructure is on the roadmap for the
next budget cycle, even a short call now helps you arrive with numbers. Which way should I go?
```

### Sequence B — B2B (Marek, Development / ESG Director)
> Tone: commercial, asset-value + ESG-reporting framing.

**Day 1 — Email 1:**
```
Subject: {Company}'s ESG report + a visible asset

Hi {Name},

For {Company}'s {development / HQ}, living walls and green roofs do double duty: verifiable
CO₂ for your ESG report and a tenant-visible differentiator that supports asset value.

We bring the install + the measurement. Worth a short call to see if it fits {project}?
```
**Day 2 — LinkedIn connect** · **Day 4 — Email 2 (case + CO₂ angle)** · **Day 7 — Call**
· **Day 11 — Break-up email.**

### Sequence C — Hospitality (Greta, Café Owner)
> Tone: short, warm, visual. This is fast and high-volume.

**Day 1 — Email / DM:**
```
Subject: turn your {rooftop/wall} into the reason people post about {Café}

Hi {Name}, we design living walls & rooftop gardens for cafés — plus branded cups to tie it
together. It becomes a signature spot people photograph and come back to. Can I send 2 quick
mockups for {Café}?
```
**Day 3 — follow-up with a visual mockup** · **Day 6 — drop by / call.**

## 4. Qualification (BANT-lite)

| | B2G | B2B | Hospitality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | Climate/adaptation budget line? | Capex for project/fit-out? | Can fund a wall + cups? |
| **Authority** | Owns or influences the budget? | Decision on the asset? | Owner = decision-maker |
| **Need** | Mandate or pressure (flood/heat/air)? | ESG target / certification? | Differentiation need? |
| **Timeline** | Budget cycle / plan deadline? | Project timeline? | Season / opening? |

A meeting is "qualified" if **≥3 of 4** are confirmed.

---

## 5. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Plants are a nice-to-have." | "Agreed if they're just decoration. We treat them as infrastructure — the model shows avoided stormwater and health costs. Can I show you the {City} number?" |
| "No budget." | "Most cities fund this from adaptation/EU climate budgets, not parks. Want me to point to which line and the funding sources?" |
| "How do I know the numbers are real?" | "Fair — the model uses published per-m² factors and it's fully adjustable. I'll send the calculator so you can change every assumption yourself." |
| "We already work with a landscaper." | "Great — we're not competing on planting. We add the value model and the regulatory framing your landscaper doesn't. Happy to complement them." |
| "Maintenance sounds like a hassle." | "We include maintenance. You get the asset and the metrics; we keep it alive." |
| "Send me an email." (brush-off) | "Will do — so I send the right thing, is {stormwater} or {ESG reporting} the bigger priority right now?" |

---

## 6. Metrics the SDR owns

- **Activity:** accounts sourced, sequences active, touches completed.
- **Pipeline:** reply rate, positive-reply rate, meetings booked, SQLs by segment.
- **Quality:** meeting-held rate, SQL→opportunity conversion, sales-cycle length per segment.
- **Feedback loop:** which trigger/message drives the best reply rate → feed back into list-building.

> Interview talking point: a good SDR is a *market-learning function*. Every sequence is an
> experiment; the win is finding which trigger + message converts, then making it repeatable.
