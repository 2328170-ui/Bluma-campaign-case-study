# Phase I–III GTM Roadmap

A phased rollout where **each phase has an entry gate, a goal, owned channels, and exit KPIs**.
Phases are sequential and self-funding: revenue and proof from one phase unlock the next.

---

## At a glance

| | Phase I — Prove | Phase II — Replicate | Phase III — Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Geography** | Lithuania (Vilnius first) | Baltics → Nordics + Poland | EU core (DE, NL, FR, AT) |
| **Goal** | Build proof + the value model | Repeat the motion in similar markets | Win mandate-driven big markets |
| **Primary motion** | Hospitality activation + 1 city pilot | B2G + B2B outbound, localized | Partner/channel + enterprise B2G |
| **Duration (illustrative)** | Months 0–6 | Months 6–18 | Months 18–36 |
| **Exit gate** | 1 reference city + 5 café sites live | 3 new-market cities signed | Multi-city framework deals |

---

## Phase I — Prove (Lithuania)

**Objective:** Generate undeniable, photogenic proof and finalize the quantified value story.

**Key actions**
- Launch the **hospitality activation** (see `07-hospitality-rollout.md`): 5 flagship café
  sites with rooftop/wall plantations + branded cups in high-footfall Vilnius locations.
- Land **one lighthouse city pilot** (a single district or set of municipal buildings).
- Ship **v1 of the impact model** and validate assumptions with real install data.
- Build the content engine: before/after visuals, a public "city savings" microsite.

**Owned channels:** local PR, café foot traffic, LinkedIn (founder + sustainability angle),
direct municipal relationships.

**Exit KPIs (gate to Phase II)**
- ≥ 1 municipal reference customer with a signed case study.
- ≥ 5 hospitality sites live and generating social proof.
- Impact model calibrated against ≥ 3 real installations.
- A repeatable 6-step sales motion documented.

---

## Phase II — Replicate (Baltics → Nordics + Poland)

**Objective:** Prove the motion travels. Same playbook, new flags.

**Key actions**
- Stand up an **outbound SDR motion** targeting city sustainability departments and
  commercial developers (see `06-sdr-playbook.md`).
- Localize the impact model per country (carbon price, energy cost, stormwater tariffs).
- Recruit 1–2 local install/maintenance partners per market.
- Run the hospitality activation as the awareness-opener in each new capital.

**Owned channels:** outbound SDR sequences, EU green-policy events/conferences,
partner co-marketing, localized microsites.

**Exit KPIs (gate to Phase III)**
- Cities signed in ≥ 3 new markets.
- Repeatable CAC and sales-cycle benchmarks established per segment.
- ≥ 2 reliable local delivery partners per active market.
- Pipeline coverage ≥ 3× the Phase III revenue target.

---

## Phase III — Scale (EU core)

**Objective:** Enter large, mandate-driven markets where green-roof regulation already exists,
and shift from one-off deals to **frameworks and channels**.

**Key actions**
- Pursue **multi-site / multi-year framework agreements** with large cities and REITs.
- Build a **partner/channel program** so BLUMA is specified by architects and developers.
- Position around existing **green-roof mandates** (several German, Dutch, French cities).
- Tie into corporate **ESG reporting** needs as a B2B wedge.

**Owned channels:** channel partners, enterprise B2G procurement, industry analyst/ESG
reporting ecosystem, paid + earned media at EU scale.

**Exit KPIs (program success)**
- Framework deals across multiple EU-core cities.
- Approach the full-scale model footprint (~3,000,000 m² → ~€148.76M modeled annual public value).
- Self-sustaining channel pipeline (majority of new deals partner-sourced).

---

## Why phasing de-risks the expansion

1. **Capital efficiency:** spend is gated on proof, not hope.
2. **Playbook reuse:** each ring is chosen for similarity, so localization cost stays low.
3. **Credibility compounding:** by the time BLUMA enters competitive EU-core markets, it
   arrives with references, a calibrated model, and partners — not a cold pitch.

## KPI dashboard (track across all phases)

- **Awareness:** microsite traffic, PR reach, social impressions from café sites.
- **Pipeline:** SQLs by segment, pipeline coverage ratio, sales-cycle length.
- **Conversion:** win rate by segment, CAC, average deal m².
- **Impact (the differentiator):** cumulative m² installed, CO₂ sequestered, € public savings —
  pulled straight from the impact model and used in every renewal/expansion conversation.
