# ICP & Buyer Personas

Three segments, each with a distinct ICP, persona, trigger, and value hook. The SDR motion in
`06-sdr-playbook.md` is built directly on these.

---

## Segment 1 — B2G (Public sector) · *primary*

**ICP (account):**
- EU city or municipality, population 100k+, with a published climate/sustainability plan.
- Under a stormwater, heat-island, or air-quality mandate.
- Has an allocated green-infrastructure or climate-adaptation budget line.

**Persona — "Daiva, Head of Urban Sustainability"**
| | |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Deputy Mayor / City Manager |
| Goals | Hit climate targets, show visible wins to citizens, defend budget |
| Pains | Targets without tools; pressure from regulation; limited capex |
| What she cares about | Public savings, compliance, citizen-visible proof |
| Hook | *"€148.76M in modeled public savings"* + a reference city |
| Objection | "Plants are nice-to-have, not budget-critical" |

**Trigger events:** new climate action plan published, EU adaptation funding awarded, a flood
or heatwave in the news, a green-roof mandate passed nearby.

---

## Segment 2 — B2B (Commercial real estate / corporate)

**ICP (account):**
- Developers, REITs, or property managers with mid-to-large urban portfolios.
- Corporates with public ESG commitments and owned/leased HQ space.
- Active in markets with green-building incentives or certifications (BREEAM/LEED).

**Persona — "Marek, Development / ESG Director"**
| | |
|---|---|
| Reports to | COO / CFO |
| Goals | Asset value, tenant attraction, ESG-report credibility |
| Pains | Greenwashing risk, certification points, differentiation |
| What he cares about | Property uplift, CO₂ for reporting, tenant appeal |
| Hook | Asset value + verifiable CO₂ + ESG-report-ready metrics |
| Objection | "What's the payback / does it lease faster?" |

**Trigger events:** new development announced, ESG report cycle, certification target,
sustainability hire, tenant sustainability demands.

---

## Segment 3 — Hospitality / SMB (awareness engine)

**ICP (account):**
- Independent or small-chain cafés/restaurants in high-footfall urban spots.
- Owners who compete on brand, vibe, and differentiation.
- Have a rooftop, courtyard, or street-facing wall.

**Persona — "Greta, Café Owner"**
| | |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Herself |
| Goals | Footfall, Instagrammability, repeat customers, standing out |
| Pains | Thin margins, sameness, seasonal dips |
| What she cares about | Looks great, draws people in, low hassle |
| Hook | A signature green wall/rooftop + branded cups = a destination |
| Objection | "Cost and maintenance hassle" |

**Trigger events:** new opening/refurb, summer season, local sustainability press, competitor
differentiation.

> Hospitality is not the revenue engine — it is the **awareness and proof engine** that makes
> the B2G and B2B conversations warmer. See `07-hospitality-rollout.md`.

---

## Prioritization (Phase-aligned)

| Priority | Segment | Why | Phase emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B2G | Largest value story, anchors the brand, mandate-driven demand | All phases |
| 2 | Hospitality | Cheap, fast, visible proof that warms the other two | Phase I lead |
| 3 | B2B | High deal size but longer cycle; benefits from B2G + visible proof | Phase II–III |
