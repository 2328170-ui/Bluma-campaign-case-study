# Executive Summary

## The opportunity

European cities are under simultaneous pressure from three trends:

1. **Climate regulation** — EU Green Deal, Fit-for-55, and national net-zero targets force
   cities to cut emissions and manage urban heat and stormwater.
2. **Urban livability** — heat islands, air quality, and flooding are now political priorities.
3. **ESG capital** — developers and corporates need credible, visible sustainability assets.

Green infrastructure (green roofs, living walls, urban vegetation) sits at the intersection
of all three. It is one of the few interventions that is simultaneously a **regulatory
compliance tool**, a **public-health asset**, and a **marketing-visible ESG statement**.

## The brand

**BLUMA** is a Lithuanian green-infrastructure brand. The product is modular green roofs and
living walls plus the design, install, and maintenance service around them. Strong in
Lithuania; effectively unknown across the rest of the EU.

## The problem we're solving

The product is good, but **awareness and a repeatable GTM motion are missing**. Green
infrastructure is bought by three very different buyers (governments, commercial developers,
and hospitality/SMBs) who each need a different message and a different proof point. BLUMA
has no segmented motion and no quantified value story.

## The strategy in one line

> **Lead with a quantified public-value story, prove it through a high-visibility hospitality
> activation, and scale market-by-market with a phased B2G + B2B motion.**

## The three moves

| Move | What | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Quantify the value** | Build an impact model translating green m² into € savings + CO₂ | Turns "nice plants" into a budget-justifiable line item for cities |
| **2. Activate visibly** | Café/hospitality rollout — rooftop & wall plantations, branded cups | Creates physical, photogenic proof in high-footfall locations |
| **3. Scale in phases** | Lithuania → Baltics → EU core, with gates between phases | De-risks expansion; each phase funds the next |

## The numbers (modeled, at full European scale)

- **~€148.76M / year** in projected government savings (stormwater, energy, health, carbon, amenity)
- **~€56M / year** in projected toll/congestion relief to commuters via green-corridor modal shift
- **~18,000 tonnes / year** of CO₂ sequestered
